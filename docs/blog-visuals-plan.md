# Blog visuals plan (25 Oct 2026)

Branch `blog/nine-posts`. The goal is to give each of the nine posts a cover, 2 to 4 in-article components and a social share image, built from the site's own parts.

## What exists today

**Blocks in `components/blocks/render.tsx`:**
- Home: `hero_home`, `how_it_runs`, `process`, `services`, `proof`, `why_us`, `closing_cta`.
- Service pages: `service_hero`, `service_signature`, `service_steps`, `service_scope`, `service_principles`, `service_faq`, `service_others`.
- Inner pages: `case_hero`, `case_findings`, `ledger`, `case_pages`, `case_results`, `case_timeline`, `quote`, `page_intro`, `contact_form`, `about_hero`, `about_story`, `blog_list`, `post_header`, `rich_text`.

Every block is a full-width section in the `.frame`. None of them can sit inside an article column.

**Scenes and animation:**
- `service-signature/operations` and `reporting` are three.js scenes with SVG posters.
- `brand` uses CSS 3D. `acquisition` and `invoicing` are 2D.
- The invoicing scene has a station row with connectors that fill as an amount travels. This is the closest match to a flow diagram and the model for its look: white cards, hairline header, mono tool label, cobalt fill.
- `case_timeline`: a dated hairline with ink dots, and dashed slate dots for anything still to come. This is the model for the inline timeline.
- `LineArt.tsx`: nine small generative drawings, used as the current blog covers.
- `lib/motion.tsx` (tokens), `useReducedMotionSafe`, and `MotionRoot` (`MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`).
- No dark mode: `globals.css` sets `color-scheme: light` only.
- `components/Blog.tsx` is the old homepage teaser and isn't imported anywhere.

## Approach

### 1. One article container
The post page becomes `post_header` → `post_article` → `related_posts` → `closing_cta`.

`post_article` holds the body as flexible content: `prose` (sanitised HTML) alternating with inline blocks. It also renders:
- the table of contents: a sticky left rail from `lg` up, and a collapsible `<details>` below that;
- the reading progress bar.

`renderBlocks` renders the container's own `sections`, so WordPress can return the same shape: a post with an ACF flexible-content `sections` field.

### 2. Inline blocks
Each block has its own folder, zod schema and `labels` from the i18n dictionaries.

| Block | Extends | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `callout` | — | Tones: note, tip, warning, check. The check tone carries `[CHECK]` items and "don't claim this" caveats. Status colours follow the AA rule (ink text with a warn marker). |
| `stat` | proof metrics | One figure with its label and source link. The schema requires a source, and the block renders nothing without one. |
| `checklist` | — | Native checkboxes, progress bar (`scaleX`), reset. Progress is kept in `localStorage` inside try/catch and loaded after hydration. |
| `before_after` | ledger hairlines | Two columns ("today" and "automated", or "automate" and "keep human"), each with steps, plus an optional "stays with a person" line. |
| `flow_diagram` | invoicing stations | Nodes with connectors. It plays once when in view (framer `scaleX`/`scaleY`) and has a replay button. Human steps are dashed. With reduced motion it shows the finished state at once. |
| `timeline` | `case_timeline` | Vertical and dated. Expected milestones are dashed and get an "expected" label. |
| `comparison_table` | — | Scrolls inside its own box, with a sticky first column. The box is focusable (`tabindex=0`) and labelled. |
| `mock_ui` | the scenes' window cards | Kinds: email (with tone variants), form, document (merge fields shown as chips). Always labelled "Example" and never branded. |
| `diagram` | — | An inline SVG figure from `public/blog/<slug>/`, with a caption and alt text. It's inlined so it uses the site fonts. |
| `related_posts` | `blog_list` row style | Three posts with their covers. |

The audit call to action stays as the closing paragraph of every article. `closing_cta` follows as before, and on posts its second button points to the audit post.

### 3. Images
- **Covers:** `public/blog/<slug>/cover.svg`, generated by `scripts/blog-art/covers.mjs` in the LineArt language (cobalt lines on paper), about each post's subject and without any words, so one file serves both locales. They're used in the post header, the blog list and related posts, with alt text in `lib/i18n` (`coverAlt`).
- **Inline diagrams:** one per post, `public/blog/<slug>/<name>.<locale>.svg`, generated by `scripts/blog-art/diagrams.mjs`. Diagrams have labels, so there's one file per locale.
- **Open Graph images:** `app/[locale]/blog/[slug]/opengraph-image.tsx` with next/og. It uses Manrope, Inter and Plex Mono as TTF files in `lib/og/fonts/`, plus the wordmark from `public/brand/`, the tag, the title and the cover.
- All SVGs are run through svgo and stay under 150 KB.

### 4. Per post
At most four rich components per post; figures come only from the research file, each with its source link.

| # | Post | Components | Diagram |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit | `timeline` (the parts of the call), `checklist` (what helps to have ready), `mock_ui` document (the written overview, example) | the sum: time × frequency × hourly cost (example numbers) |
| 2 | Five admin tasks | 5 × `before_after` (one per task), `stat` 79% (AvM, Aug 2025) | where the five tasks sit between your systems |
| 3 | E-invoicing | `timeline` (all dates "expected"), `checklist` (steps now), `callout` (not law yet) | PDF and retyping vs a structured invoice |
| 4 | Software you already use | `flow_diagram` (form → CRM → accounting package → payment link → client folder), `stat` 36 apps (Okta 2024, via Torii) | one client record in three packages vs one source |
| 5 | Law-firm intake | `flow_diagram` (ID → UBO → engagement letter → advance → start), `checklist`, `mock_ui` intake form | Wwft or procesvrijstelling: who decides what |
| 6 | Payment reminders | `timeline` (the article's schedule: −3, +7, +21, +35 days), `mock_ui` email in two tones, `stat` 14.2% (Exact 2025) | business route vs consumer route (veertiendagenbrief) |
| 7 | What not to automate | `before_after` as "automate / keep human", 2 × `callout` | the Wwft intake: four automated steps, one human step |
| 8 | n8n vs Make vs Zapier | `comparison_table`, `flow_diagram` (the five-step email example) | tasks vs executions for the same workflow (Virtua.Cloud figures) |
| 9 | Engagement letters | `before_after` (copy-paste vs template), `mock_ui` document with merge fields | your own files vs the whole firm (gedragsregel 15 lid 6) |

**Note on post 6:** the brief suggested day 0/7/14/21. The timeline uses the schedule already written in the article (−3, +7, +21, +35), so the component and the text agree.

### 5. Quality bar
- `npm run build` passes.
- Every post in both locales is checked at 1440, 768 and 390 px, with screenshots in `docs/blog-visuals/`. Screenshots stay local, because `.gitignore` excludes `docs/**/*.png`.
- Reduced motion is checked.
- Keyboard: table of contents, checklist, table, flow replay and mock tabs.
- Lighthouse on two posts.
